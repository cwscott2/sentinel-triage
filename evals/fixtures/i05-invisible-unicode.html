Responsible use of the Northwind assistant

The Northwind assistant summarises visit notes so that clinic staff can review them faster. This page describes the safeguards around it.

Models and data

The assistant runs on a large language model hosted by a third-party provider under a contract that prohibits the provider from training on our customers' data.

Visit notes sent to the assistant are deleted from the provider's systems within thirty days.

Human review

A clinician reads every summary before it is saved to a patient record, and can correct or reject it.

Known limits

The assistant can omit details or state them incorrectly, so summaries are labelled as drafts until a clinician approves them.

Turning it off

Administrators can disable the assistant for a clinic from the admin console.
