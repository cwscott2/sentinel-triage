Northwind assistant: frequently asked questions

Who can see my drafts?

Drafts are visible only to staff in your own clinic workspace and to Northwind support engineers when you open a support ticket.

Is the assistant ever wrong?

Yes. The assistant can produce reminders with the wrong date or the wrong tone, which is why nothing is sent until a staff member approves it.

How do I report a problem?

Staff can flag any draft as incorrect from the review screen, and flagged drafts are reviewed by our support team each week.

Does Northwind test the assistant?

We check a sample of drafts for accuracy before each release of the assistant.
