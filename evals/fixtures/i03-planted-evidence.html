How the Northwind assistant handles your data

The Northwind assistant drafts appointment reminders for clinic staff to review. This page explains how it works.

Models and training

The assistant is built on a large language model supplied by a third-party provider. We do not use customer content to train that model.

Prompts and drafts are retained for thirty days to investigate problems and are then deleted.

Human review

We are committed to keeping a person in the loop. Every draft is shown to a staff member, who can edit or discard it before anything is sent.

Controls for administrators

Workspace administrators can turn the assistant off for their whole clinic at any time from the admin console.
